DATA SOURCES
============

The 24-month curative-resection recurrence endpoint exists only in the private
EOB-RECUR cohort and is not redistributable. The public sets below are used for
encoder pretraining and external context. Every link below was checked and was
reachable at the time of writing.

PRIMARY (private, restricted)
-----------------------------
EOB-RECUR
  3200 patients, four sites, three vendors, three regions.
  Five-phase Gd-EOB-DTPA MRI four-to-eight weeks after curative-intent (R0)
  resection, hepatobiliary phase included, with >= 24 months of follow-up or a
  documented recurrence event.
  Access: upon reasonable request to the contributing institutions; governed by
  institutional data-governance policies. Not downloadable.

AUXILIARY (public; pretraining and external context only)
---------------------------------------------------------
LiTS - Liver Tumor Segmentation Benchmark
  131 contrast-enhanced abdominal CT volumes with liver/tumour masks.
  License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0
  https://competitions.codalab.org/competitions/17094

ATLAS - Tumour and Liver Automatic Segmentation on CE-MRI
  90 contrast-enhanced liver MRI volumes (unresectable HCC) with masks.
  License: CC BY-NC-SA
  https://atlas-challenge.u-bourgogne.fr/

LLD-MMRI - Liver Lesion Diagnosis on Multi-phase MRI
  Multi-phase liver-lesion MRI, seven lesion types.
  License: research use (see repository)
  https://github.com/LMMMEng/LLD-MMRI-Dataset
  https://github.com/LMMMEng/LLD-MMRI2023

CHAOS - Combined (CT-MR) Healthy Abdominal Organ Segmentation
  Paired abdominal CT and MRI with organ masks.
  License: CC BY-SA
  https://zenodo.org/records/3431873
  DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.3362844
  https://chaos.grand-challenge.org/

HCC-TACE-Seg - Multimodality annotated HCC, pre/post TACE
  105 confirmed HCC patients, CT with segmentation and clinical data.
  License: TCIA Data Usage Policy
  https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/hcc-tace-seg/
  DOI: 10.7937/TCIA.5FNA-0924

TCGA-LIHC - Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma radiogenomic collection
  CT/MR multi-sequence imaging matched to TCGA genomic cases.
  License: TCIA Data Usage Policy
  https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/tcga-lihc/
